LRCDF all-in-one app. Everything you need is included and it works offline.

Get started
  1. Drag LRCDF.app to Applications, or keep it in another folder.
  2. Double-click it. First-time setup takes about a minute. It copies the app and models to ~/LRCDF (about 2.7 GB) and installs the plug-in in Lightroom Classic.
  3. Open Lightroom Classic. If it's already open, quit and reopen it.
  4. Select your photos and choose Library → Plug-in Extras → Start One-Click Retouch….

If macOS says it can't verify the developer, or that Apple could not check the app for malicious software
  The app has no Apple developer signature, so you allow it once by hand:
  1. In the message, click Done (not Move to Trash).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the note about LRCDF, click Open Anyway and enter your Mac's login password when asked.
  3. Double-click LRCDF.app again and click Open Anyway in the confirmation.
  Open Anyway only appears for about an hour after you tried to open the app; if you don't see it, double-click the app again. You only need to do this once.
  (Since macOS 15, right-click → Open no longer skips this step.)

What you need
  - An Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 26 or later, and Lightroom Classic.
  - 6 GB of free space (about 2.7 GB for the app and 2.7 GB for the installed files).

Licenses and privacy
  - Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
  - After installation, see ~/LRCDF/project/legal/ for the licenses and terms.

To update later, replace the old LRCDF.app with the new one and double-click it. The app updates itself.
